\section{Summary}
%======================================================================
\begin{itemize}
  \item The time term of $\Theta_{\rm reg}$ used so far (\code{legacy}) is uninformative. It is built from the conditional time
        score $\tau$ of Guth et al., evaluated on walkers that are independent of the noise $Z$, so it targets a constant
        (Section~\ref{sec:indep}). Every past $\Theta_{\rm reg}$, including the bimodal REG figures (A8), was smoothed toward that target.
  \item New energy \code{moment} (now the default with \code{--reg\_solver thomas}). Its time term is time-score matching on the
        walker law, whose normal equations are exact by the MGD constraint alone (A4):
        $\hat\Sigma_w\dot\Theta=-\hat{\dot m}$ (\MGD\ sign). It uses no $\tau$, costs nothing extra per step, and can be
        rebuilt offline for old runs.
  \item Implemented B1--B8 (Section~\ref{sec:impl}). Unit and end-to-end tests pass. The legacy path is bit-identical to 4baf4c2
        (Section~\ref{sec:tests}).
  \item Validation in 1-D against a Fokker--Planck ground truth (C1, C2) and a check of the per-step noise model (B4):
        Section~\ref{sec:valid}. C3 (existing turbulence systems) needs Jean Zay; the commands are in Section~\ref{sec:c3}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Corrections (30 Sep review)}\label{sec:corr}
%======================================================================
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.2em,style=nextline]
\item[A1. Algorithm~1 corrector sign: closed.]
  The PDF has the same line as the HTML, $x_{k+1}=y_k+h\hat\theta^\top\nabla\phi(y_k)$. It is a typo in the paper, wrong in both
  the sign and the missing $\sigma^2$ \ext. The correct corrector is MGD eq.~(19), $X_{t+h}=Y-h\sigma^2\hat\theta^\top\nabla\phi(Y)$
  (\MGD\ sign), with $\hat\theta$ from eq.~(21) \ext. The code follows eq.~(19) \ver\ (\code{x\_k\_plus\_one = y\_k + theta\_raw.grad phi},
  $\theta_{\rm raw}=h\sigma^2\theta^{\rm code}=-h\sigma^2\theta$). All notes now cite eqs.~(19)/(21), not Algorithm~1.
\item[A2. Numbering.] In the project copy: $D_{\rm KL}(p^\sigma_1\|p_*)\le C\sigma^{-2}$ is Conjecture~4.1 (eq.~25); $\frac{d}{dt}H\ge\theta^\top\dot m$
  is Proposition~4.3 (eqs.~28--29); the entropy bound is Conjecture~4.4 (eq.~32) \ext. The arXiv HTML version I read
  numbers them Conj.~4.3 / Prop.~4.4 / Conj.~4.5; this note uses the project-copy numbers. ``Nothing is claimed for
  intermediate $t$'' was too strong: the formal $\sigma\to\infty$ limit (eqs.~22--24) holds for all $t$, and only the conjecture is
  stated at $t=1$ \ext.
\item[A3. The premise of the old \S3.1 was wrong; its conclusion stands.]
  The MGD constraint is $\E[\phi(X_t)]=m_t$ for the walker law $p^\sigma_t$ (eqs.~12, 17), not $\E_{p_{\theta_t}}[\phi]=m_t$ \ext.
  MGD's $\theta_t$ solves $G_t\theta_t=\E[\Delta\phi(X_t)]$ (eq.~15, \MGD\ sign): it is the Fisher (score-matching) projection of
  $p^\sigma_t$ onto the family, with the gap given by eq.~(31) \ext. The identity $\Sigma_\theta\dot\theta=-\dot m$ holds for the
  max-entropy multiplier $\theta^{\rm ME}(m_t)$, which equals $\theta_t$ only formally as $\sigma\to\infty$. Re-derived \ver. Numerically, in 1-D
  (bimodal, Fokker--Planck ground truth), $\|\theta_t-\theta^{\rm ME}\|_G/\|\theta^{\rm ME}\|_G=1.35\cdot10^{-3}$ at $\sigma=10$ and
  $3.6\cdot10^{-2}$ at $\sigma=2$ \ver. The sign and centring conclusion (legacy targets $K\dot\Theta=-\E[\phi\tau]$ with the wrong sign and uncentred) stands.
\item[A4. The proposed P2 is exact; it is the draft's eq.~(6).]
  Take time-score matching on $p^\sigma_t$ with model time score $-\dot\Theta^\top\phi+c_t$, where $c_t$ is free (\MGD\ sign). Its normal equations are
  \[\Sigma_w\dot\Theta=-\Cov_w\big(\phi,\partial_t\log p^\sigma_t\big)\quad\text{and}\quad
    \Cov_w(\phi,\partial_t\log p^\sigma_t)=\tfrac{d}{dt}\E_w[\phi]=\dot m_t\]
  by the constraint alone. Re-derived \ver\ ($\E_w[\partial_t\log p^\sigma_t]=0$ and $\frac{d}{dt}\E_w\phi=\E_w[\phi\,\partial_t\log p^\sigma_t]$).
  It is the draft's eq.~(6), derived in its Appendix \ext. Consequences:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item ``unbiased when $\Sigma_w=\Sigma_\theta$'' and ``not a TSM on a single law'' are deleted;
    \item the old open question~1 is answered: $C=\hat\Sigma_w$;
    \item empirically the identity holds to $O(h)$, because the corrector enforces the moment condition on walker averages \ext.
  \end{itemize}
  What remains is a \emph{projection mismatch}. The data term targets the Fisher projection of $p^\sigma_t$ ($\theta_t$); the time term
  targets its time-score projection. They coincide only when $p^\sigma_t$ is in the family (formally $\sigma\to\infty$). The earlier wording
  calling the dynamics model ``nearly exact'' is withdrawn. The $\tau$-based modes were a mis-implementation of the draft's eq.~(4) \ext.
\item[A5. The old C5 was not a test of MGD's $\theta_t$.]
  In \code{verify\_bias.py}, $\theta$ is obtained by moment matching, i.e.\ it is $\theta^{\rm ME}$. The $10^{-10}$ row checks the
  quadrature and the finite differences only \ver. Restated conclusion: the interpolant-side TSM projects the time score of the
  \emph{interpolant} law $p_t$, while the data term projects the score of $p^\sigma_t$. It mixes two laws. The recommendation to drop it stands.
\item[A6. Guth invariance (old open question 4): resolved; $\lambda$ stays free.]
  (a) Guth's loss written in \code{Cos} time is invariant. Data rescaling $X\to cX$ gives $Y'=c\,Y_\beta$ with $\tan\beta=c\tan\alpha$, which is
  Guth's rescaling. I checked this algebra \ver: $Y'=cX+\cot\alpha\,Z=c(X+\cot\beta\,Z)$. The improper $p(s)$ is not a new problem:
  $w_D=2\dot\alpha\cos\alpha/(d\sin\alpha)\approx2/(dt)$ as $t\to0$ \ver, the same log divergence as $\int ds/s$, with $t_1$ in the role of $s_{\max}$ \ext.
  (b) Our estimator is not invariant at finite $\sigma$, whatever the weights. The rescaling acts as a time reparametrisation plus a
  time-dependent dilation, which turns constant-$\sigma$ MGD into MGD with a time-dependent effective volatility. Invariance
  returns only formally as $\sigma\to\infty$, and only for homogeneous $\phi_k$ \ext. So invariance does not justify a fixed trade-off.
  The $w_D$, $w_T$ formulas (with the $\sin^2\alpha$ fix) were re-derived by the review and are correct \ext; they were already checked here \ver.
\item[A7. Entropy and $\log\mathcal Z$: keep the per-step $\theta$.]
  Replacing $\theta$ by $\Theta_{\rm reg}$ inside $H^*$ (MGD eq.~30) or the draft's $\log\mathcal Z$ bound (eq.~8) loses the lower-bound guarantee.
  In the \MGD\ sign, $\frac{d}{dt}H-\Theta^\top\dot m=(\theta-\Theta)^\top\dot m+\sigma^2\cdot(\text{Fisher gap})$, and the first term has no sign \ext.
  The per-step noise averages out in these integrals anyway. With the B4 noise model,
  $\mathrm{Var}\big(\sum_kh\,\hat\theta_k^\top\dot m\big)\approx\frac{2}{n\sigma^2}\int_0^1\dot m^\top G^{-1}\dot m\,dt$, finite as $h\to0$
  (derivation \ver, given B4).\newline
  \emph{Current use in the code} \ver\ (\code{codes/utils\_entropy.py} and the notebooks calling it): $H^*$ always integrates the per-step
  \code{dH\_t\_bound}; $\Theta_{\rm reg}$ enters only as the end point $\theta_1$ in $\log\mathcal Z\ge H^*+\theta_1^{\rm code}\cdot m_1$
  (\code{log\_Z\_bound(..., 'Theta\_reg')}; \code{jets/jets\_KL\_divergence.ipynb}; \code{turbulence/turb\_KL\_divergence.ipynb};
  \code{theta\_interpretation/}). By Gibbs' inequality, $\log\mathcal Z_\vartheta\ge H(q)-\vartheta^\top\E_q\phi$ holds for \emph{any} $\vartheta$
  (\MGD\ sign; $q=p^\sigma_1$, $H(q)\ge H^*$). So that end-point use is still a valid lower bound, but on $\log\mathcal Z$ at $\vartheta=\Theta_{\rm reg}(1)$,
  a different model from the one at $\theta_1$ \ver\ (derivation). Recommendation: per-step $\theta$ for $H^*$ and $\log\mathcal Z$;
  $\Theta_{\rm reg}$ for $\theta(t)$ and $\theta_1$ as a coupling/interpretation object.\newline
  \emph{Side finding} \ver: \code{compute\_log\_Z\_sigma} in \code{jets/jets\_KL\_divergence.ipynb} computes $H-\theta_1^{\rm code}\cdot m_1$, while
  \code{utils\_entropy.log\_Z\_bound} (and the derivation, \cs\ sign) gives $H+\theta_1^{\rm code}\cdot m_1$. The notebook is not edited.
\item[A8. Draft Fig.~1 (``regularised MGD, 2 orders of magnitude''): flagged for re-run.]
  I cannot see the draft. The bimodal REG pipeline is the candidate: \code{bimodal\_experiment/kl\_bimodal.ipynb} (\code{KL\_bimodal\_paper\_1.png},

\section{Notation}
%======================================================================
\paragraph{MGD} (Lempereur et al., arXiv:2602.17211; equation numbers of the project copy \ext).
$x\in\R^d$; statistics $\phi:\R^d\to\R^r$; $Z\sim\mathcal N(0,\Id)$, $X\sim p$; interpolant $I_t=\cos\alpha_t\,Z+\sin\alpha_t\,X$,
\code{Cos} schedule $\alpha_t=\pi t/2$, $\dot\alpha=\pi/2$. Target moments $m_t=\E[\phi(I_t)]$. Walkers $X_t\sim p^\sigma_t$, constraint
$\E[\phi(X_t)]=m_t$ (eqs.~12, 17). Gram $G_t=\E[\nabla\phi(X_t)\nabla\phi(X_t)^\top]$.
\MGD\ sign: $G_t\eta_t=\dot m_t$, $G_t\theta_t=\E[\Delta\phi(X_t)]$ (eq.~15); SDE $dX_t=(\eta_t-\sigma^2\theta_t)^\top\nabla\phi\,dt+\sqrt2\sigma\,dW_t$,
$X_0=Z$; Fokker--Planck form eq.~(22). Corrector eqs.~(19), (21).
\paragraph{Two multipliers} (\MGD\ sign). $\theta_t$: MGD's, the Fisher projection of $p^\sigma_t$ (eq.~15). $\theta^{\rm ME}(m)$: the max-entropy
multiplier, $\E_{p_{\theta^{\rm ME}}}[\phi]=m$. They are equal formally as $\sigma\to\infty$.
\paragraph{Guth et al.} (arXiv:2506.05310). Their noise variance ``$t$'' is written $s$ here. $y=x+\mathcal N(0,s\Id)$, $p\propto e^{-U}$;
$\ell=\E_{s}\big[\frac sd\ell_{\rm DSM}+(\frac sd)^2\ell_{\rm TSM}\big]$ with $p(s)\propto1/s$ (their eq.~8); $\partial_s$ in $\ell_{\rm TSM}$ at fixed $y$.
\paragraph{Other symbols.} $K=\E[\phi\phi^\top]$, $\Sigma=\Cov(\phi)$; subscripts $w$ (walker empirical law), $I$ (interpolant); hats are Monte-Carlo
estimates. $n$ walkers, SDE step $h=t_{k+1}-t_k$, nodes $t_k$ of the regularised problem, $\dt_k$ node spacing, $h_k$ trapezoid weights.
$\tau(x;Z)=-\dot\alpha[\tan\alpha(d-\|Z\|^2)+Z^\top X]$, $X=(x-\cos\alpha Z)/\sin\alpha$: minus the conditional time score of $p_t(x|X)$ (sign-free).

\section{The estimators as implemented}\label{sec:impl}
%======================================================================
\subsection{Per-step $\hat\theta_k$ and its noise (B4)}
\cs\ sign: $(\hat M_k+\varepsilon D_k)\,\hat\theta^{\rm code}_k=\hat b_k$, $D_k=\operatorname{diag}\hat M_k$, $\varepsilon=$\code{regularization}; potentials below
\code{\_live\_floor} are set to 0. \MGD\ sign: $(\hat M_k+\varepsilon D_k)\hat\theta_k=\hat\beta_k\approx\E[\Delta\phi]$ (eq.~21).
\begin{claim}[noise model, \ext; derivation \ver; holds numerically at $\sigma=2$, Table~\ref{tab:B4}]
$\Cov(\hat\theta_k)\approx\frac{2}{nh\sigma^2}\hat M_k^{-1}$, white in $k$.
\end{claim}
Derivation: given $x_k$, the Brownian increment makes $\bar\phi(y_k)$ fluctuate with covariance $\approx\frac{2h\sigma^2}{n}\hat M_k$.
Dividing by $h\sigma^2$ gives $\Cov(\hat\beta_k)\approx\frac{2}{nh\sigma^2}\hat M_k$, hence the claim. It is white because each corrector step resets the walker
averages to $\hat m_{t_{k+1}}$, so no fluctuation carries over to the next step.

\subsection{Regularised energy}
\cs\ sign (what \code{\_solve\_regularised\_thomas} minimises; weights from \code{reg\_energy\_weights}):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:energy}
\mathcal E[\Theta]=\sum_ka_k\Big(\tfrac12\Theta_k^\top\hat M_k\Theta_k-\hat b_k^\top\Theta_k\Big)
+\sum_{k<N}\Big(\tfrac{q_k}2\Delta\Theta_k^\top C_k\Delta\Theta_k+l_k\,\tilde c_k^\top\Delta\Theta_k\Big).
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Moment mode, both signs.} Per cell, the moment time term is $\lambda w_T\dt[\frac12\dot\Theta_c^\top C\dot\Theta_c-\dot m^\top\dot\Theta_c]$ (\cs),
minimised at $C\dot\Theta^{\rm code}=\dot m$. With $\Theta=-\Theta^{\rm code}$ this is $C\dot\Theta=-\dot m$ (\MGD), the draft's eq.~(6). Checked by
unit test T2: exact to $O(\dt^2)$ for every $\lambda\in[0,10^4]$, and the flipped sign fails \ver.

\paragraph{Interpretation (B4), \ext; to be checked.} With the quadrature weights $a_k=h_k$ and the noise model, the data term is
$\frac{2}{n\sigma^2}$ times the Gaussian negative log-likelihood of the $\hat\theta_k$:
$\sum_kh_k\frac12(\Theta_k-\hat\theta_k)^\top\hat M_k(\Theta_k-\hat\theta_k)=\frac{2}{n\sigma^2}\sum_k\frac{nh_k\sigma^2}{4}(\dots)$ (up to a constant) \ver\ (algebra).
The time term is then a Gaussian prior on the residual $r=\hat\Sigma_w\dot\Theta+\hat{\dot m}$ (\MGD\ sign) with precision $\lambda n\sigma^2/2$ in the
$\hat\Sigma_w^{-1}$ metric. Consequence used in the selection: $\chi^2(\lambda)=\sum_k\frac{nh_k\sigma^2}{2}(\hat\theta_k-\Theta_k)^\top\hat M_k(\hat\theta_k-\Theta_k)/N_{\rm live}$
should be $\lesssim1$ when $\lambda$ removes only noise.

\paragraph{Projection mismatch (A4).} In the moment mode the data term pulls toward $\theta_t$ (Fisher projection) and the time term toward the
time-score projection of $p^\sigma_t$. These differ unless $p^\sigma_t$ is in the family, so large $\lambda$ trades one for the other. This is the only
bias of the moment energy with exact inputs.

\subsection{What was implemented (B1--B8)}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.2em,style=nextline]
\item[B1] \code{interp\_time\_terms} is off by default and exposed as \code{--interp\_time\_terms}. $\hat K_I$ is no longer saved; if on, only the $r$-vector
  diagnostics \code{diag\_c\_I}, \code{diag\_m\_I}, \code{diag\_tau\_mean\_I} are.
\item[B2] Mode \code{moment} (\code{--reg\_mode}; default \code{moment} with \code{--reg\_solver thomas}, \code{legacy} with \code{dense}; the config name gets
  \code{\_moment}). $\hat\Sigma_w$ is taken at the moment-matched walkers $x_{k+1}$; $\hat{\dot m}$ is the pathwise \code{dt\_phi\_I\_k}, already
  computed for the predictor. There is no $\tau$ anywhere. Old systems: $\hat\Sigma=\hat K_w-\hat m\hat m^\top$ with $\hat m=$\code{barphi\_p}, and
  $\hat{\dot m}$ from a 3-point non-uniform finite difference of \code{barphi\_e} (row $j$ at $t_{j+1}$) (\code{resolve\_theta\_reg.py --mode moment
  --results\_root}). Caveat: there $\hat K_w$ is float32 and at $y_k$.
\item[B3] $\hat\Sigma_w$ accumulated centred in float64. It is stored float32, like $\hat M$, so the offline re-solve reproduces the in-run one exactly (F3).
\item[B4] Quadrature weights by default in the moment mode (table above); the noise-model interpretation above; the numerical check in Table~\ref{tab:B4} (holds at $\sigma=2$).
\item[B5] Dead-statistic masking in the Thomas solve: identity row and column, right-hand side 0 (\code{live} argument; unit test T3). New runs save
  the per-step mask. Old runs: \code{--mask theta} rebuilds it from the saved $\theta_t$, which the corrector set to exactly 0 (F7); \code{--mask diag}

\item[B6] $w_D=2\dot\alpha\cos\alpha/(d\sin\alpha)$ (T5). The Guth schedule is \code{--reg\_schedule guth} of the moment mode; the $\tau$-based
  \code{fixed}/\code{guth} modes of 29 Sep are removed.
\item[B7] \code{forward\_regularised} docstring and assert message corrected; the walker $\tau$ is computed only in legacy mode.
\item[B8] \code{codes/lam\_select\_cv.py}, via \code{resolve\_theta\_reg.py --select} (\code{SELECT=1}). It computes a held-out moment-matching loss
  (fit on even nodes, score the data term on odd nodes; equal up to a constant to $\frac12\sum h_k\|\hat M_k\Theta_k-\hat b_k\|^2_{\hat M_k^{-1}}$, the
  linearised moment mismatch), an amplitude veto ($\max_k|\Theta_{k,i}|>10A_i$, with $A_i$ from block medians of $\Theta(0)$), and $\chi^2(\lambda)$.
  The whole path $\Theta(\lambda)$ is saved.
\end{description}

\section{Audit findings that stand}
%======================================================================
\subsection{The walkers are independent of $Z$}\label{sec:indep}
\code{run\_experiment} passes neither \code{x\_0} nor \code{x\_k} to \code{SDE}, so they are separate \code{torch.randn} draws
(\code{codes/sde\_routines.py}, \code{init\_interpolants\_and\_workers}) \ver. With $Z\perp x$, $\E[\tau|x]=\dot\alpha d\cot\alpha$ is a constant, so
$\Cov_w(\phi,\tau)=0$ and $\E_w[\phi\tau]=\dot\alpha d\cot\alpha\,m_w$ (sign-free) \ver\ (derivation; \code{verify\_claims.py} C2: $\E_w\tau=77.28$/25.12/8.12 vs.\
77.35/25.13/8.17). Starting the walkers at $Z$ (the bimodal port) does not help (C3 of \code{verify\_claims.py}) \ver.

\subsection{$\tau$ on true pairs, and the interpolant path}
On the interpolant, $\E_I[\tau]=0$ and $\E_I[\phi\tau]=-\dot m$ (sign-free), so $-\hat c_I$ is a score-function estimate of $\dot m$. It is 3.5--21$\times$
noisier than the pathwise $\hat{\dot m}$ ($d=16$, \code{verify\_claims.py} C1) \ver. The interpolant TSM mixes two laws (A5).

\subsection{Other}
The ridge equivalence $(\hat M+\varepsilon\operatorname{diag}\hat M)^{-1}$ is exact (F7 reproduces $\theta_t$ to $3\cdot10^{-7}$) \ver. Legacy weights are
unweighted sums, so on non-uniform grids the node density weights the energy (derivation \ver); the moment mode uses quadrature. Centring a
float32 $\hat K$ loses precision (toy: 7\% error on a variance at mean/std $=1000$) \ver, which is why B3 accumulates centred.
